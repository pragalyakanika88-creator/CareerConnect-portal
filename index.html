About CareerConnect Portal

Choosing a career path is one of the most critical decisions school students make. Many students feel overwhelmed by conflicting advice, complex eligibility criteria, or lack of reliable guidance. CareerConnect Portal provides high school students with straightforward, transparent career insights—including realistic indicative salaries, course durations, and entrance examination roadmaps in India.
